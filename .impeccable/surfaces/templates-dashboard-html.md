## Direction contract

THESIS: Every saved video is a field-guide entry — organized by family, identified by compact marks — refusing the generic white-card SaaS grid every AI dashboard defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Cream field-guide ground (#f3ede0) on near-black ink (#1c1a16), one family-green accent (#7a8b5c) for active topic tabs, one rust accent (#b5542f) reserved only for flagged/actionable state. Condensed sans for entries, hairline rules, zero border-radius, color-coded edge tabs per topic group.

STORY: Open the dashboard and instantly know which family (topic) has something worth attention; entries read fast by terse ID marks (category, score, status) without opening each one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A left-edge tab rail (topic groups) at full height; a dense entry list fills the rest — thumbnail, title, 2-3 terse fields per row, score rendered as a filled bar, not a number.

FORM: Field Guide, candidate 6 of 7 (assigned by roll), seed key 07e8fd5a, re-roll round 1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
