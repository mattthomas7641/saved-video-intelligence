## Direction contract

THESIS: Your library is a guide map, not a spreadsheet — eight topics as flat, fully-saturated territories you navigate between; refuses both the generic SaaS dashboard AND the muted single-hue "reskin" this replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Flat unmodulated color, zero gradients/shadows. Cream-white ground (#F6F1E3), near-black ink (#22231F). Eight topic territories each own one full-strength flat hue (azure, gold, violet, coral, magenta, teal, green, warm-gray for Other) — color marks WHERE you are, applied at the map/nav layer and as the current topic's accent, never scattered per-row across a dense list. One grotesque typeface (Archivo) throughout. Controls are full capsules (999px radius) — a reversal of hairline/zero-radius, earned by this world, not arbitrary.

STORY: Land on a map of eight colored territories sized by how much they hold; pick one, and its color becomes the whole view's accent while you scan a dense, fast, mostly-monochrome list inside it — color never fights the data for attention once you're reading rows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The library overview is eight flat-color territory tiles (not bordered gray cards), sized/weighted by video count, each naming its topic and top pick. Entering a topic swaps the accent to that territory's color across rail, active tab, and score bars.

FORM: Guide Map, bolder-register hand, challenger id challenger-zoo-map, seed key 07e8fd5a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
