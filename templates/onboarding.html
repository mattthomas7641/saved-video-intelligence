Import your saved videos

TikTok has no way to read your Saved list directly, so we start from your own data export. It stays on this computer.

1. Ask TikTok for your data

  1. In the TikTok app, open Profile → menu → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data.
  2. Choose JSON as the file format, then request the data.
  3. When it's ready (minutes to a few days), download it and unzip. You want user_data_tiktok.json.

2. Upload it here

Only the links to your saved videos are used. Re-importing is safe: videos you already have are skipped.
